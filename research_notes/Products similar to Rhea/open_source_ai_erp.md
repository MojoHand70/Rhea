# Open-source AI + ERP / business-system projects resembling Rhea (survey as of October 2026)

Rhea's six-point signature used for assessment: (1) AI authors business rules as versioned *data* (strict JSON), never code; (2) draft → human-approved → active lifecycle; (3) deterministic kernel over an append-only event log; (4) replay through same rule versions reproduces identical state; (5) per-field provenance (event id, rule id, rule version); (6) domain knowledge shipped as data packs.

**Headline takeaway: no open-source project found combines all six properties. The nearest neighbors each cover a different fragment:** GoRules ZEN (rules as deterministic JSON data + AI copilot + human-gated release, no event log), Accounted (agent + staged human approval + append-only accounting records, but AI operates rather than authors and logic is code), AiNativeERP (draft-confirm-execute governance of AI *intents*, not rules), and erp-dsl (ERP-as-language for LLM authorship, embryonic).

## Q1: What other open-source "AI-native ERP" / "AI accounting" / "AI business OS" projects exist beyond lambda-erp and Open Mercato?

### Takeaway
A small but active 2025–2026 cohort exists — Accounted (398★), ERPClaw (110★), AutoForceAI (26★), AiNativeERP (7★), OpenConstructionERP, ai-workforce — plus the much larger no-code platform NocoBase (21.6k★) with "AI employees". In every one, the AI *operates* or *assists* the system through natural language or MCP tools; none has the AI *author versioned rules-as-data* that a deterministic kernel then executes.

### Cited Findings
- The GitHub topic `ai-erp` lists 6 repos: avansaber/erpclaw (110★, Python, updated Oct 5 2026), AndyLinGo/AutoForceAI (26★, TypeScript), Edison0621/AiNativeERP (7★, C#), arunrajiah/ai-workforce (4★, "AI-native apps that run entire departments"), sanadmobile/AutoForceAI (1★), mohitraj8503/Vyapaari-Plus (0★, "AI-first Business Operating System for Indian retailers") — [GitHub topic ai-erp](https://github.com/topics/ai-erp)
- **Accounted** (erp-mafia/accounted): "The Open Source ERP Built for Agents" — 398★, AGPL-3.0, Next.js 16/React 19/TypeScript/Supabase (PostgreSQL + RLS), 2,259 commits, active CI. "The full bookkeeping engine is exposed as 150+ MCP tools with scoped API keys or OAuth, so an AI agent can do the books." "Posting is staged for human approval, so the agent proposes and you decide." "Voucher immutability, sequential voucher numbering, period locks, and 7-year document retention are enforced by database triggers, not by convention." Append-only document archive with SHA-256 integrity. AI side uses Anthropic SDK on Bedrock or compatible endpoints; connects to Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini over OAuth 2.1 — [erp-mafia/accounted](https://github.com/erp-mafia/accounted)
- **ERPClaw** (avansaber/erpclaw): 110★, GPL v3, Python, 103 commits, 46 modules across 14 accounting domains. "AI-native from the first commit, the assistant as the primary interface" — plain-English operations ("invoice Wayne Industries for 10 widgets at $100"). Every operation routes through one entry point `scripts/db_query.py --action {action}`; money stored as TEXT/Python Decimal, never floats; "GL entries are immutable; a cancellation posts reversing entries, never an edit"; industry verticals fetched on demand via sparse checkout from `github.com/avansaber/*` repos with `validate-module` and constitution checks. Approvals explicitly incomplete: "they do not yet require a second approver, and we will not call them a control until they are one"; agent loop ("Atrium") is planned proprietary — [avansaber/erpclaw](https://github.com/avansaber/erpclaw)
- **AiNativeERP** (Edison0621/AiNativeERP): 7★, MIT, .NET 10 + React, 22 recent commits; 7 domain agents, 28+ governed tools, RAG. Pipeline: "LLM generates candidate intent → Application Service validates → write operations produce drafts → users confirm before execution." Tools are deterministic predefined operations with risk classification; "write operations must be refusable, confirmable, and replayable"; Agent Trace logging + audit logs, but no explicit event sourcing — [Edison0621/AiNativeERP](https://github.com/Edison0621/AiNativeERP)
- **AutoForceAI** (AndyLinGo/AutoForceAI, 26★, TypeScript): "open source digital employees platform that deeply integrates LLM, RPA, GEO, Knowledge Base (RAG), and Business Systems (BI, ERP, CRM, eCommerce)" — [GitHub topic ai-erp](https://github.com/topics/ai-erp)
- **OpenConstructionERP** (datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP): AGPL-3.0 construction ERP with "AI cost matching, 293 guided cases, 120,000+ priced positions, 45 languages, 192 modules" — notable mainly for shipping large domain content as data, though its AI is matching/ML, not rule authorship — [datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP](https://github.com/datadrivenconstruction/OpenConstructionERP)
- **NocoBase** (21.6k★): "AI employees" are AI roles with defined permissions that operate inside business workflows, "automatically generate follow up records and write them directly into the system," participate in approval workflows under access controls, pluggable LLM backends. The article does not claim AI defines or modifies business configuration — [NocoBase blog: NocoBase vs Odoo vs ERPNext](https://www.nocobase.com/en/blog/top-3-open-source-erp-with-ai-on-github-nocobase-vs-odoo-vs-erpnext)
- **Proprietary category marker (not open source):** DualEntry is "an AI-native ERP built from day one around AI-powered accounting" (SaaS) — [Numeric: AI accounting software roundup](https://www.numeric.io/blog/ai-accounting-software)
- **ERPClaw's own marketing site** describes it as "AI-native ERP: accounting, inventory, sales, purchasing… operated in plain language. Free forever, self-hosted. GPL v3." — [erpclaw.ai](https://www.erpclaw.ai/)

### Per-candidate Rhea comparison (closest resemblance vs sharpest difference)
- **Accounted** — Closest: agent-proposes/human-approves staging, append-only immutable vouchers with cryptographic integrity, compliance "by construction" in the data layer. Sharpest difference: the AI *operates* the books through fixed MCP tools; business logic is TypeScript code plus DB triggers, never AI-authored data; no rule versioning, no replay-determinism claim, no per-field rule provenance.
- **ERPClaw** — Closest: AI-as-primary-interface over a strict accounting kernel with immutability and no-floats discipline; vertical modules fetched as packages echo market packs. Sharpest difference: all logic is Python code; modules are code packs, not data packs; no event log/replay; the agent executes actions rather than drafting rules; approval controls admitted incomplete.
- **AiNativeERP** — Closest: explicit draft → validate → human-confirm → execute lifecycle and "refusable, confirmable, replayable" writes. Sharpest difference: what the LLM drafts are *transactions/intents*, not rules; business rules live in Application Services code; replay means audit-record review, not deterministic state reconstruction.
- **NocoBase** — Closest: logic-as-configuration platform (no-code) where AI acts under permissioned roles inside approval workflows. Sharpest difference: configuration is human-authored; AI executes within workflows; no event sourcing, no provenance model.
- **AutoForceAI / ai-workforce / Vyapaari-Plus** — chat/RPA-style digital employees; no rules-as-data, no event log; listed only for completeness.
- **lambda-erp** (known): "Open-source AI-native ERP… all through chat," "configurable in plain language"; the project frames ERP implementation as "text-transformation and configuration that LLMs are now good at" — [lambdadevelopment/lambda-erp](https://github.com/lambdadevelopment/lambda-erp)
- **Open Mercato** (known): positions itself as an "AI-Engineering Foundation Framework" where "Cursor, Claude Code and Codex build features instead of re-deciding architecture" — i.e., AI authors *code at development time*, the inverse of Rhea's runtime rules-as-data; their blog argues "'AI features' are not enough" for open-source ERP — [openmercato.com blog](https://www.openmercato.com/blog-posts/open-source-erp-alternatives)

### Inferences
- The entire 2025–2026 open-source AI-ERP cohort converges on one architecture: deterministic hand-written tool layer + LLM as operator with human approval of *transactions*. Rhea's move — human approval of *rules*, with transactions then flowing deterministically — appears unoccupied.
- Accounted is the most mature and philosophically closest on invariants (append-only, human gate, agent proposes), making it the best "compare and contrast" reference for positioning.

### Gaps
- Star counts come from page fetches on 2026-10-05 and may be approximate; `ai-workforce`, `Vyapaari-Plus`, and `sanadmobile/AutoForceAI` were not individually inspected (negligible traction).
- No Show HN/Hacker News post surfacing an event-sourced, rule-authoring AI ERP was found; HN-targeted searches returned mostly commercial AI-accounting content. Absence of evidence, not proof of absence.

## Q2: Have the established open-source ERPs added AI layers, and do any author business logic as data with approval?

### Takeaway
Odoo (v19, Sept 2025) and Axelor now ship built-in AI agents, ERPNext has a third-party AI app ecosystem, and NocoBase has "AI employees" — but in all of them AI assists or executes; none lets AI author business logic/rules as versioned data behind an approval lifecycle. Tryton, iDempiere, and Metasfresh have no AI; Dolibarr has only basic AI-assisted reports.

### Cited Findings
- **Odoo 19** (49.1k★) released September 2025 with an AI application: configurable RAG agents ("an agent is built from topics — instructions plus the tools it may use — and sources: documents, Knowledge articles, links or PDFs"), AI fields, and an "Ask AI" assistant; providers are Gemini and OpenAI — [Odoo 19 release notes](https://www.odoo.com/odoo-19-release-notes); [Nalios: Odoo 19 AI agents with RAG](https://www.nalios.com/en/blog/what-s-new-in-odoo-6/odoo-19-ai-deploying-your-own-intelligent-agents-with-rag-123); [Pixelmechanics: Odoo 19 new features](https://www.pixelmechanics.tech/en/blog/odoo-19-new-features-2025)
- Odoo's AI is characterized as a "supplementary tool rather than integrated system agent… no indication of AI defining business logic; mainly content generation and analytics support" — [NocoBase comparison blog](https://www.nocobase.com/en/blog/top-3-open-source-erp-with-ai-on-github-nocobase-vs-odoo-vs-erpnext)
- **ERPNext** (31.9k★): "doesn't come with native AI features out-of-the-box"; AI arrives via community apps — changAI (ERPGulf: natural-language querying of business data, optional voice via Amazon Polly), NextAI (Gemini-based content generation: terms & conditions, grammar), Frappe Assistant Core (MCP server for workflows/insights/analytics) — [Frappe forum: AI in ERPNext](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/ai-artificial-intelligence-in-erpnext/146995); [ERPGulf/changai](https://github.com/ERPGulf/changai); [erpnextai/next_ai](https://github.com/erpnextai/next_ai); [Frappe Assistant Core](https://mcpmarket.com/es/server/frappe-assistant-core)
- **Axelor** now markets itself as "Open Source Agentic ERP with Built-in AI Agents"; its platform is "The Open Source ERP, CRM & BPM platform powered by AI and Low Code" with a visual BPMN 2.0 studio, 30+ business apps, and the claim that "for 70-80% of standard ERP customizations, you don't write Java. You configure." AI is described as automating recurring tasks — [axelor.com](https://axelor.com/); [Axelor no-code BPM](https://axelor.com/no-code-bpm/); [Ecosire: Odoo vs Axelor 2026](https://ecosire.com/blog/odoo-vs-axelor-comparison-2026)
- **Tryton** "has no AI capabilities"; **iDempiere, Metasfresh** and related Java systems "have no AI capabilities"; **Dolibarr** 2025 added only "basic AI-assisted reports," with AI capabilities otherwise "essentially none" — [Ecosire: top 10 open-source ERP 2026](https://ecosire.com/blog/open-source-erp-top-10-comparison-2026)

### Inferences
- The incumbents' AI layers are uniformly assistive (RAG chat, content generation, NL querying) or task-automating. The closest structural analogue to "logic as data" among them is not AI at all: Odoo agent *configuration* (topics/tools/sources as records), Axelor's BPMN-as-configuration, and Frappe's DocType metadata — all human-authored, with no draft/approve lifecycle for AI output and no event-sourced execution underneath (all mutate ORM state in place).
- Because incumbents keep mutable ORM state, they structurally cannot offer Rhea's replay determinism or per-field provenance without a rewrite — a defensible moat claim.

### Gaps
- Could not verify whether Axelor's "AI agents" can create or modify BPMN process definitions themselves (marketing pages describe task automation only; no technical doc found stating AI authors workflows).
- Odoo Experience 2025 session details (whether agents can perform write actions beyond configured tools) were not verified from a primary Odoo source beyond the release notes summary.

## Q3: Are there open-source projects where an LLM generates declarative rules/configuration (not code) that a deterministic engine executes — in any business domain?

### Takeaway
Yes — this exists as a category, centered on business-rules engines rather than ERPs. The strongest example is GoRules ZEN (2k★, MIT): business logic as a portable JSON Decision Model executed deterministically, with an AI copilot + MCP server that edits rules and runs tests but "never deploys" — release stays with human reviewers. That is Rhea's criteria 1–2 nearly verbatim, minus the event log, replay, provenance, and ERP domain.

### Cited Findings
- **GoRules ZEN** (gorules/zen): 2,000★, MIT, Rust core with Node.js/Python/Go native bindings and Java/Kotlin/.NET via UniFFI, 612 commits. Its JSON Decision Model (JDM) stores "business logic as portable JSON" — decision tables, switches, expressions, functions, reusable sub-decisions — and "decisions evaluate in microseconds, run identically on every platform." Rule storage is left to the host ("file system, database or service call") — [gorules/zen](https://github.com/gorules/zen)
- GoRules (the platform around ZEN) ships "an AI copilot and MCP server that edits rules, runs tests and explains decisions" but "never deploys" — releases remain with human reviewers, with "a paper trail for every change" and "instant rollback"; marketed as "reviewed, versioned, and explainable forever" — [gorules.io](https://gorules.io/); [gorules/zen](https://github.com/gorules/zen)
- **DecisionRules AI Assistant** "turns plain-language policies into working Decision Tables, functions, and test data" with governed approval workflows — but DecisionRules is a commercial SaaS, not open source — [DecisionRules blog](https://www.decisionrules.io/en/articles/ai-for-business-rules-from-plain-language-to-working-logic/)
- **lwardzala/business_rules_reasoning** (Python): "a logical reasoning system combining rule-based engines with LLMs for business automation… deduction and hypothesis testing, partial fact completion, automated fact retrieval via documents or chat… explainable reasoning, workflow integration, customizable knowledge bases"; rules defined as JSON (predicates, operators, conclusions) — [lwardzala/business_rules_reasoning](https://github.com/lwardzala/business_rules_reasoning)
- Pre-LLM substrate the category builds on: CacheControl/json-rules-engine ("a rules engine expressed in JSON"), venmo/business-rules ("Python DSL for business intelligence rules that can be configured without code"), kenolo ("lightweight JSON-based business rules engine") — [CacheControl/json-rules-engine](https://github.com/CacheControl/json-rules-engine); [venmo/business-rules](https://github.com/venmo/business-rules); [nire0510/kenolo](https://github.com/nire0510/kenolo)
- Academic framing of the same split: "Blueprint First, Model Second" — "an expert-defined operational procedure is first codified into a source code-based Execution Blueprint, which is then executed by a deterministic engine," decoupling workflow logic from the generative model — [arXiv 2508.02721](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.02721)
- A Medium walkthrough describes building an "Agentic AI Business Rules Engine for Java… lets business users write rules in plain English with deterministic execution and audit trails" as an LLM-powered alternative to Drools — tutorial-level, not a maintained project — [Medium: Agentic AI Business Rules Engine for Java](https://medium.com/@visrow/agentic-ai-business-rules-engine-for-java-build-your-own-llm-powered-alternative-to-drools-2eea47f22e7c)
- In the DMN world (Drools, Camunda), decision tables are declarative and deterministic, but searches found no open-source LLM-generates-DMN tool; prior automated generation used decision trees (Weka) mapped to DMN rules, not LLMs — [Camunda DMN docs](https://docs.camunda.io/docs/8.7/guides/create-decision-tables-using-dmn/); [DMN decision tables semantics, arXiv 1603.07466](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.07466)
- Adjacent architecture essay: "The Log Is the Agent" (Yohei Nakajima / BabyAGI lineage) proposes "making the append-only event log the source of truth, and deriving all agent state from that log… every interaction is an append-only event, and each consumer — LLM loop, UI, persistence — receives a projection that suits its contract" — event sourcing applied to agent infrastructure, not to business rules — [Developers Digest: The Log Is the Agent](https://www.developersdigest.tech/blog/log-is-the-agent-event-sourced-ai)

### Per-candidate Rhea comparison
- **GoRules ZEN/JDM** — Closest: the single nearest architectural neighbor found anywhere — logic as versioned JSON data, deterministic cross-platform evaluation, AI authors/edits but humans alone release, full change paper trail. Sharpest difference: it is a decision engine, not a system of record — no append-only business event log, no replay of state, no per-field provenance on resulting business objects, no domain packs; and the AI copilot belongs to the commercial platform layer, not the MIT engine.
- **business_rules_reasoning** — Closest: JSON rules + LLM + explainability in one loop. Sharpest difference: the LLM participates at *inference time* (completing facts, retrieving data), undermining determinism — the exact thing Rhea's kernel/agent split forbids; no versioning/approval lifecycle.
- **"The Log Is the Agent"** — Closest: append-only log as sole source of truth with per-consumer projections, Rhea invariants 1/4 in spirit. Sharpest difference: an essay/pattern about agent state, not an ERP, no rules, no human approval dimension.

### Inferences
- Rhea's criterion 3+4 combination (deterministic kernel *over an event log*, replay-verified) is what separates it from the whole BRMS+AI category: GoRules-style systems make *decisions* reproducible but not *state*; event-sourcing projects make state reproducible but have no AI-authored rules. No project found that fuses the two.
- The BRMS vendors (GoRules, DecisionRules, Progress, ruleengine.ai) independently converged on Rhea's governance stance — "AI proposes, humans release" — which validates the pattern commercially while leaving the open-source, event-sourced, ERP-domain instantiation open.

### Gaps
- No open-source LLM→DMN generator found despite targeted search; Camunda/Red Hat may have proprietary copilots not surfaced here.
- The GoRules AI copilot's exact mechanics (whether output is strictly schema-validated JDM JSON) are described only in marketing copy; not verified against code since the copilot is closed-source.

## Q4: Any projects claiming "ERP as a language/DSL" or "business logic as data" with AI authorship?

### Takeaway
Exactly one direct claim found: jjaim519/erp-dsl, a DSL explicitly designed as "a user manual for LLMs" acting as "an assembly compiler for ERP no-code builders," where the LLM converts natural language into validated schemas. It is tiny and obscure. The nearest established analogues are human-authored logic-as-data platforms (Frappe DocTypes, Axelor BPMN, NocoBase plugins) that AI can at most fill in, not define.

### Cited Findings
- **jjaim519/erp-dsl**: "a DSL designed as a user manual for LLMs as an assembly compiler for ERP no-code builders. The LLM's role is to convert natural language into validated schemas and assemble components from a specified catalog" — [jjaim519/erp-dsl](https://github.com/jjaim519/erp-dsl)
- Open Mercato's framing is the other "language for AI" claim in the ERP space, but aimed at code generation: an "AI-Engineering Foundation Framework designed so that Cursor, Claude Code and Codex build features instead of re-deciding architecture" — [lambda-erp/Open Mercato search context](https://github.com/lambdadevelopment/lambda-erp); [openmercato.com](https://www.openmercato.com/blog-posts/open-source-erp-alternatives)
- ERPNext/Frappe's architecture is the long-standing "ERP definitions as data" precedent (DocTypes = metadata-driven models), and community threads discuss AI strategy, but AI integration remains "via APIs or custom applications… no native business logic modification" — [NocoBase comparison blog](https://www.nocobase.com/en/blog/top-3-open-source-erp-with-ai-on-github-nocobase-vs-odoo-vs-erpnext); [Frappe forum AI strategy thread](https://discuss.frappe.io/t/ai-chatgpt-strategy-for-erpnext/103098)
- Historical note: "an open source ERP system built to self-implement" (opensource.com, 2014) shows the "self-building ERP" phrase predates LLMs and meant guided configuration, not AI authorship — [opensource.com](https://opensource.com/business/14/11/building-open-source-erp)
- Commercial BRMS marketing has adopted the exact language: "Conversational AI can turn natural-language policy into typed, validated, and human-approved business rules" (DecisionRules); Progress markets "Business Rules Management for AI"; ruleengine.ai sells an "AI Rule Engine" — all closed source — [DecisionRules](https://www.decisionrules.io/en/articles/ai-for-business-rules-from-plain-language-to-working-logic/); [Progress BRM](https://www.progress.com/data-platform/capabilities/business-rules-management); [ruleengine.ai](https://www.ruleengine.ai/)

### Inferences
- "ERP as a language with AI authorship" is essentially unclaimed territory in open source: erp-dsl gestures at it (LLM → validated schema → component assembly) but has no kernel, no events, no lifecycle, and no visible traction. Rhea's specific synthesis — language + AI author with approval + deterministic replayable kernel + provenance + packs — has no open-source peer as of October 2026.
- Where fragments exist, they split cleanly by community: BRMS people have rules-as-data and AI authoring; event-sourcing people have append-only logs and replay; ERP people have domain packs (Odoo localizations, ERPNext regional apps) and now agent operators. The fusion is the novelty.

### Gaps
- erp-dsl was identified only from a search snippet; its README, stars (likely ~0), and activity were not fetched — treat its description as self-reported.
- No search was run specifically over HN Algolia or awesome-event-sourcing lists; a niche prototype could have been missed, though five differently-phrased searches surfaced none.
