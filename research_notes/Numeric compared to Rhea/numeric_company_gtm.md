# Numeric (numeric.io) — company and go-to-market, as of 2026-10-08

Scope note: this covers Numeric Technologies, Inc. (brand "Numeric", numeric.io), San Francisco, CEO Parker Gilbert. Three unrelated entities with similar names appear in search results and must not be conflated: "Numeric Technologies" of Warrenville, Illinois (an SAP/IT services firm founded 1996), "Numerix LLC" of New York (a 2023 breach notice belongs to it), and Polish firms "Numeris Sp. z o.o." / "Numeratio Sp. z o.o." (unrelated). All dates below are the date of the source or the dated event; "estimate" marks aggregator figures that no primary source confirms.

Headline for the Rhea comparison: Numeric began (2020) as an AI close-management layer on top of NetSuite-class ERPs, but on 2026-09-22 it launched the "Financial Data Platform" (FDP), explicitly marketed as an ERP replacement whose architecture is "business events stored as complete records, with debits and credits as one projection" and rules ("flows") drafted by AI and approved by controllers. That is close to Rhea's thesis, but Numeric targets US enterprise/mid-market tech companies past ~$100M ARR with no visible non-US localisation (no VAT, e-invoicing, IFRS or Polish/CEE presence found).

## Founding story: founders, backgrounds, year, original thesis

### Takeaway
Three Duke classmates (Parker Gilbert, Andrew Bihl, Anthony Alvernaz) founded the company in 2020 (some sources say 2021) after Gilbert's experience as the first finance hire at Hearth; it was renamed from "Velocity" to "Numeric" in 2022. The original thesis was AI-assisted automation of the month-end close for in-house accounting teams.

## Stated vision and roadmap: layer on ERPs, or replace the ERP?

### Takeaway
Numeric has moved decisively from "close layer on top of your ERP" to "replace NetSuite": the 2025-11 Series B framed a "compound startup" / "connected platform for finance operations by 2027", and on 2026-09-22 it launched the Financial Data Platform as an "ERP replacement built for the agent era", with an event-sourced data model (business events as complete records; debits/credits as a projection), AI-drafted accounting rules approved by controllers, and an incremental migration path ("an on-ramp, not a cliff"). Gilbert dismisses AI-native GL rivals as "same house, new paint".

### Cited Findings
- Series B (2025-11-19), Gilbert: "We started by solving the close, but our vision has always been greater"; company will launch "additional products throughout 2026 and 2027, each automating a different accounting workflow"; three live products: Close Management, Analytics, Cash Management — [PR Newswire Series B](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/numeric-raises-51m-series-b-expanding-from-close-management-to-comprehensive-finance-platform-302619774.html)
