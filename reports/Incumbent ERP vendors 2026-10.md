# Incumbent ERP vendors, October 2026

Research for the target catalog of packs (`PACKS.md`), 2026-10-23. Companion to "AI-native ERP
newcomers 2026-10" and "Products similar to Rhea". Every number is the vendor's own claim unless
said otherwise; prices from third-party guides are estimates; [n] points to the sources at the end.

## 1. Global vendors

**SAP**
- **What they sell:** since 2025 the Business Suite is sold as "cloud suite packages" by line of business — lead-to-cash, procure-to-pay, record-to-report [4]; S/4HANA Cloud through RISE (large firms) and GROW (midmarket) [5].
- **Business One:** 83,000+ customers, 850+ partners [6]. **ByDesign:** partners report it leaves the price list in April 2026 with S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition as successor; SAP published no end date (uncertain) [7].
- **AI:** Joule with 30+ agents and 2,500+ skills [1] (the German edition of the release says 40+ and 2,400+); an AI Agent Hub for governance [1]. Joule needs a RISE or GROW contract, on-premise excluded [5]. A DSAG survey: 3% of SAP customers use SAP Business AI productively [2]. Joule Studio (custom agents) slipped from Dec 2025 to Q3 2026 [3].
- **Extensibility:** BTP, under a "clean core" rule [8]. **Implementation:** GROW partners advertise fixed-scope go-lives of 4–12 weeks against classic 12+ month projects [8].
- **Poland:** KSeF through partner add-ons (All for One, Key Group) [9]; no SAP-native KSeF found for Business One (uncertain).

**Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP**
- Financials, Procurement, Project Management, Risk, EPM, priced per named user; about $175 per user per month list per module, typical discounts 40–65% (estimates) [13].
- Prebuilt agents "at no additional cost" since Oct 2025 [10]; AI Agent Studio to build, test and deploy agents, choose the model, add human-approval steps (e.g. Payables, Cash Processing agents) [12]; the AI Agent Marketplace opened with 100+ partner agents — an agent built from a partner template is custom AI with a separate Oracle fee [11].

**Oracle NetSuite**
- Core suite plus modules sold separately; a typical midmarket deal (core + 4–8 modules + 25–100 users) about $30–75k a year (Vendr estimate) [16]; 43,000+ customers (secondary) [15].
- NetSuite Next (Oct 2025), "AI-first", agentic workflows for payment proposals and reconciliations; Ask Oracle respects roles and permissions; rollout "in the coming year" [14]. Customers build "SuiteAgents"; a SuiteApp.AI marketplace [14].
- **Poland:** JPK and KSeF only from partners (Staria's SuiteApp, RSM's localisation) [17].

**IFS Cloud**
- One platform for ERP, plant maintenance (EAM) and field service; strongest in aerospace and defence, energy, utilities, manufacturing, construction [21]. Bought Softeon (WMS) in Mar 2026; H1 2026 recurring revenue +25%, 84% of revenue [18].
- IFS.ai, Nexus Black [19], IFS Loops "Digital Workers" with an Agent Studio [20]; claims 60% of agentic transactions run fully automated; ERP Today flags execution risks [18].
- Quote-only; a guide estimates £150–300 per user per month [21]. **Poland:** IFS's own documentation sends KSeF through a third party (Pagero) [22].

**Microsoft Dynamics 365**
- Business Central $80 (Essentials) or $110 (Premium) per user per month since Nov 2025 [24]; D365 Finance about $210 (third-party) [27].
- Business Central 2026 wave 1: Sales Order Agent (email to quote to order, after a person approves), Payables Agent (invoices to purchase orders), Expense Agent in preview; agents in the online version only [23]. Custom agents with Agent Designer, or Copilot Studio through BC's MCP server [25]. D365 Finance: Account Reconciliation Agent GA Sept 2026 [28]. AppSource and the partner channel.
- **Poland:** BC's KSeF and JPK from partners (IT.integro, Companial) [26]; in D365 Finance, KSeF numbers in vendor payment journals reach preview Nov 2026 and GA Mar 2027 — a year after the mandate [29].

**Infor CloudSuite** — separate suites per industry; Industry AI Agents (Oct 2025, AWS Bedrock); customers build their own in the GenAI Agent Factory [30]; 100+ agents and MCP connectivity per a secondary source (uncertain) [30].

**Epicor (Kinetic, Prophet 21)** — supply-chain industries; Prism vertical agents in the UK and Europe (Jun 2026), ANZ and Latin America (Aug 2026), conversational access to live data and "pre-approved actions with human oversight" under "Cognitive ERP" [31].

**Sage (Intacct, X3)** — Sage Copilot across Intacct, X3, Sage 50 and others, 40,000+ customers using it [32]; Intacct Close, AP, Time and Assurance agents, a Finance Intelligence Agent due later in 2026; bought Doyen AI; an "AI Trust Label" [32]. X3 for firms of 50–2,500 staff; its CEE localisation by partners did not name Poland (2011) [33]. Sage owned Symfonia from 2005 until Mid Europa Partners in 2021 [49].

**Workday Financials** — finance agents (Sept 2025): Cost & Profitability, Financial Close, Financial Test (fraud); bought Flowise (its custom-agent builder) and Sana, whose self-service agent is sold through Flex Credits [34].

**Unit4 ERPx** — midmarket, people-centric organisations; the Ava agent in Teams orchestrates ERP workflows, aiming to automate half of all ERP tasks; AI free until Aug 2027 for customers signing by 31 Dec 2026; European model providers planned for AI sovereignty [35].

**Odoo** — 50 main apps, 50,000+ community apps, 21,000+ partners, 28M users [36]; Odoo 19 (Sept 2025) added AI agents acting in the database and natural-language search (per a partner) [37]; Standard and Custom plans (Custom adds Studio, multi-company, API), prices varying by region up to 7× [38]; a partner says the licence is about 20% of the total budget [39]. **Poland:** the official localisation includes KSeF ("E-Invoicing FA(3)") and JPK [40].

**Acumatica** — editions by industry, pricing by resources with unlimited users, sold by partners (third-party sources) [41]; AI agents and assistants for cross-selling and order orchestration (Summit 2026) [42].

## 2. Polish vendors

**Comarch** (Optima, ERP XL, Enterprise, XT) — taken private by the Filipiak family and CVC, squeeze-out Dec 2024 [43]; "over 70,000 firms keep their books in Optima" [44]; Optima's accounting-office module: VAT, declarations and JPK, operator time reports, document exchange with clients [45]; the "cloud" is a hosted private environment per named user and per office module, with robotic automation, AI and ChatERP as free add-ons [46]; ChatERP still BETA in Optima 2026, answering questions about the data and performing selected tasks, prompts sent to Comarch and OpenAI [47]. IDC figures relayed by Comarch: 24.3% of the Polish ERP market in 2023; 52.7% of small and 26.8% of medium firms [48].

**Symfonia** — owned by MidEuropa (2021) and Accel-KKR (2023); bought companies in Romania and Austria, Nefeni (systems in 25% of Polish local governments) and inEwi [49]; about 250m PLN revenue in FY2024, ~90% recurring [50]; AI only a beta chat in eDokumenty (Aug 2026) answering how-to questions from the manuals [51]; KSeF Plus free until 31 Dec 2026, limits from 2027 [52].

**enova365 (Soneta)** — 22,000+ companies, 250+ partners; SaaS or on-premise; HR and payroll, finance, CRM, BI, production; KSeF "included in the licence"; an "AI assistant" for data validation, documents and analysis [53].

**InsERT** — the older GT desktop line and nexo/nexo PRO: Subiekt (sales), Rewizor (full books), Rachmistrz (KPiR), Gratyfikant (payroll), Gestor (CRM), Biuro nexo for offices; office subscriptions priced by client companies served; 1M+ licences; no AI mentioned [54].

**Asseco Business Solutions** — Softlab (mid/large), Merit, WAPRO (small firms and offices) [55]; KSeF lifted the backlog to 374.8m PLN in H1 2025 [56]; a record H1 2026, analysts asking whether the KSeF effect is fading [57].

**Online tools** — **wFirma:** about 4,000 offices serving 130,000+ businesses; office features (clients, simplified and full books, payroll, a client portal); OCR and KSeF without limits; AI ("Magic Box", "CAK") recognises files and books expenses [58]. **inFakt** (Visma since 2020): an online accounting service with 800+ accountants of its own; KSeF; customers use their account from inside Claude or ChatGPT [59][60]. **SaldeoSMART** (Visma since 2019): document capture and OCR, approval workflow, invoicing, KSeF; 300,000+ users, about 6,300 firms and offices, about 3,000 offices in 2024 [61][62]. **mOrganizer finansów** (mBank): free invoicing with KSeF, paid packages from 10 zł a month with KPiR and JPK; an outside accountant sees approved documents only [63].

## 4. How incumbents package, sell and position AI

- **Packaging:** modules and editions per named user; line-of-business bundles (SAP), industry editions (Infor, Acumatica, IFS); marketplaces for the long tail — AppSource, SuiteApp, Odoo's 50,000 apps, Oracle's agent marketplace [11][36].
- **Selling:** almost everyone through partners — SAP B1 850+, Odoo 21,000+, enova365 250+ [6][36][53]; Polish legal depth in the global products comes from partners too.
- **AI:** an assistant and agents laid over the transactional logic that exists, within existing roles, people approving [12][14][31]; several pieces late or in beta (Joule Studio, ChatERP, Symfonia's chat, NetSuite Next) [3][47][51][14]; every global vendor lets customers build their own agents, no Polish vendor found that does; AI used to pull customers to the cloud [5][23]. Gartner warns of "agent washing" and expects over 40% of agentic-AI projects cancelled by 2027 [64].

## 5. Weaknesses an AI-native entrant can exploit

1. **Implementation time and cost** — a median of 9 months, over a quarter over budget (Panorama 2026, relayed) [65]; the licence about 20% of the total budget (an Odoo partner) [39]; SAP's "fast" route a fixed-scope 4–12 week project [8].
2. **AI bolted on and little used** — 3% of SAP customers productive [2]; agents answer and draft, the logic underneath unchanged; no vendor found claims deterministic replay, provenance on every booked fact, or versioned rules — Rhea's kernel, open ground.
3. **Price and packaging for SMEs** — per user and per module, regional prices up to 7× apart (Odoo), partner-built agents billed extra (Oracle), AI only with a cloud contract [38][11][5].
4. **Polish legal depth** — global vendors reach KSeF and JPK through partners, or late (BC via IT.integro, NetSuite via Staria and RSM, IFS via Pagero, D365's KSeF payment numbers in Mar 2027) [26][17][22][29]; the Polish vendors have the depth on older architecture (desktop GT, a "cloud" that is hosted desktop software) [46][54].
5. **Accounting offices as a channel** — office tooling in three layers: bookkeeping software per client (Optima, Rewizor, Symfonia, enova365, WAPRO), document capture (Saldeo), client portals and online services (wFirma, inFakt, mOrganizer); Visma consolidating the second and third [60][62]; no product found uniting all three on one ledger with AI that knows Polish law.

## 6. The Polish market

- **Size and shares:** IDC puts the Polish ERP market at $462M in 2024 (secondary) [48]; the last public shares, 2022: SAP 30.4%, Comarch 22.5–23.6% (sources differ), Asseco 8.8% [66][48]; GUS: 40.5% of firms with 10+ staff use an ERP, 97.2% of Poland's 2.37M non-financial enterprises are micro-firms [48].
- **Sole traders:** online tools and services — wFirma, inFakt, iFirma (2025 revenue 63.4m PLN [67]), mOrganizer for mBank customers. KSeF reaches micro-firms (up to 10k PLN of invoices a month) on 1 Jan 2027, penalties from 2027 [68].
- **Accounting offices:** multi-client software (Optima's office module, InsERT Biuro/Rewizor nexo PRO, Symfonia, enova365, WAPRO Fakir) [45][54], with a document layer (Saldeo, about 3,000 offices) or a cloud tool (wFirma, about 4,000 offices) [62][58]; no independent survey of what offices use; Symfonia's own ranking lists Symfonia, Rewizor nexo PRO, Optima, enova365, WAPRO Fakir [69]; the number of offices in Poland uncertain.
- **SMEs:** Comarch leads among small firms (52.7%) [48]; then Optima (70k firms), InsERT (1M+ licences), enova365 (22k), Symfonia. **Mid-market:** Comarch XL/Enterprise (26.8% of medium firms [48]), Symfonia ERP, Asseco Softlab, enova365, BC and SAP B1 via partners. **Enterprise:** SAP leads [66], then Oracle, IFS, D365, KSeF by add-ons [9][22][29].
- **Ownership:** private equity and consolidators — CVC (Comarch), MidEuropa and Accel-KKR (Symfonia), Visma (inFakt, Saldeo) [43][49][60][62].
