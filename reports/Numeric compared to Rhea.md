# Numeric Claims the Story, Not the Ground

Numeric's Financial Data Platform, launched on 2026-09-22, does not make Rhea pointless, but it ends one comfortable belief: that no funded company was building an event-first system of record in which AI drafts the accounting rules, a human approves them and code executes them. Numeric now says exactly that in public, with **$89M raised**, "hundreds" of close-product customers and **one named FDP customer** ([Yahoo Finance copy of the launch release, 2026-09-22](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-130000623.html); [PR Newswire, 2025-11-19](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/numeric-raises-51m-series-b-expanding-from-close-management-to-comprehensive-finance-platform-302619774.html)). What Numeric has not done, in any public document found as of 2026-10-08, is claim the properties that make Rhea's version checkable: an append-only log enforced by tests, rules stored as versioned data with the human approval recorded as an event, replay that reproduces identical state, provenance on every derived field to the rule version that produced it, and statutory law shipped as swappable data packs. And Numeric has not touched Rhea's market: the FDP is a US-GAAP replacement for NetSuite aimed at companies past roughly **$100M ARR**, the close product sells at a median of about **$15,000 a year** to US tech controllers, and no Numeric page mentions VAT, e-invoicing, IFRS 15 or any Polish ledger ([Numeric blog, 2026-02-27](https://numeric.io/blog/rillet-vs-campfire); [Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/numeric)). Rhea therefore still makes sense on precisely two grounds and no others: a verifiable mechanism rather than a claimed architecture, and the small-and-mid EU business whose statutory layer is data. The honest price of that verdict is that both grounds are today a thesis with tests, not a product with users: Rhea has no customers, a fake e-invoicing client, no bank adapter, no arithmetic, no bank matching and no recorded live demo. Numeric's existence should change three things for Rhea's owner: the pitch moves from "the architecture" to "the demonstrable mechanism plus the Polish wedge"; Numeric's "on-ramp, not a cliff" migration is a lesson to borrow rather than a threat; and the next 90 days belong to the three items that convert the thesis into field evidence — arithmetic, bank matching and two or three design partners found through an accounting office.

## Three products sit in two layers, and only one of them has users

The comparison is really three things, and conflating them produces both the fear and the false comfort. **Numeric's original close product** (the company was founded in 2020 as an AI close-management vendor) is an overlay: it reads a general ledger that already exists in NetSuite (transaction-level, every one to two minutes), or at trial-balance level in Xero, QuickBooks Online and Sage Intacct, and writes back only journal entries, only to NetSuite, only after human approval ([Numeric blog, 2026-06-23](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-vs-ledge); [Numeric JE automation page](https://www.numeric.io/product/journal-entry-automation)). Its modules are a controller's month-end: close checklist, account reconciliation with auto-submit under a materiality threshold, AI-drafted flux explanations, transaction monitors, cash matching, journal-entry automation, and since 2025–2026 billing and revenue recognition ([numeric.io pricing, fetched 2026-10-08](https://www.numeric.io/pricing)). Independent reviewers through mid-2026 all file it in the FloQast/BlackLine box; ERP Research lists it under "ERP add-ons", AgentLink's September 2026 map puts it in the retrospective close lane, and Gartner's 2025 Financial Close and Consolidation Magic Quadrant does not include it at all ([ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/financial-close/numeric); [AgentLink, 2026-09](https://www.agentlink.finance/research/ai-tools-for-cpa-firms); [Gartner abstract](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/6295715)).

**Numeric's Financial Data Platform** is a different product sold under the same brand. The press release says it "stores each business event as a complete record, including the original source document and its metadata", that "debits and credits are one view derived from that record, not the record itself", and that it is "closer to a data warehouse with accounting logic than to a typical ERP" ([Yahoo Finance, 2026-09-22](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-130000623.html)). Rules are called "flows"; agents draft the accounting treatment for a new ledger, "controllers approve and refine them", and entries are then "generated monthly through hard-coded rules" ([Accounting Today, 2026-09-25](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-to-replace-erps)). CEO Parker Gilbert told Accounting Today the FDP "is specifically a replacement for NetSuite", covering GL, subledgers, billing, revenue, cash, close and reporting but not CRM or inventory, and that the migration is "an on-ramp, not a cliff" because customers keep posting to NetSuite until they are ready. It is sold only as the full suite, custom-priced on transaction volume. Everything above is the vendor's description; the only named FDP customer is Built (Controller Asia McKnight), every launch article traces to the same release, and no analyst has assessed it ([itbrief, 2026-09-28](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-to-replace-erps)).

**Rhea** is neither of those. It is an experimental system of record whose design document states the three-part split as the founding thesis: a small language of ERP (events, objects, documents, activities, rules, views), an AI layer that authors rules "with a human in the loop", and "a deterministic kernel that executes rules, enforces invariants, and is the only thing that ever changes state. AI output is data, never code" ([SPEC.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/SPEC.md)). Seven invariants are enforced by tests rather than asserted in prose, and the project is explicitly "not a product": no auth, no multi-tenant, no performance work ([CLAUDE.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/CLAUDE.md)). Its stated endgame is "thousands of smaller and bigger EU businesses", Poland first, free with monetisation deferred ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). It has no customers; the public site is a gate "opening Rhea to a small circle first" ([rheamktg index](/home/krzysztof/rheamktg/public/index.html)).

## The earlier map survives on evidence, but its blind spot was exactly here

The October 2026 landscape report concluded that across roughly sixty systems "every one covers at most three of Rhea's six properties, and no two of them cover complementary halves in a single shipped system" ([Products similar to Rhea](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/reports/Products%20similar%20to%20Rhea.md)). That report never examined close-management vendors; its only mention of Numeric is a citation of a Numeric blog post about DualEntry, and the word does not appear in Rhea's repository at all ([open_source_ai_erp.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/research_notes/Products%20similar%20to%20Rhea/open_source_ai_erp.md)). Numeric was therefore never placed on the map, which is why the FDP launch felt like an ambush. Re-running the six dimensions against both Numeric products gives this picture:

| Rhea dimension | Numeric close product | Numeric FDP (vendor claims, 2026-09/10) | Rhea (verified by) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI authors rules as versioned data | AI suggests cash-matching rules from manual matches; monitors drafted from natural language; no versioning stated | Agents propose treatments and draft "flows"; "Approve once and every record like it follows suit"; representation undisclosed, possibly generated code; versioning claimed only for App Studio apps | Strict-JSON rule rows, new version per change; `TestDraftRuleRejectsBadOutput`; agent package has no store access |
| Draft → approved → active → superseded, approval recorded as an event | Preparer/reviewer gate at posting time; task events logged | Controller approves rules; approval is a workflow event; no rule lifecycle states published | Lifecycle gates tested (`TestApproveRejectsNonDraft`, `TestActivityLifecycleGate`); approval is an event with actor |
| Deterministic kernel over an append-only log | Warehouses NetSuite's GL; "immutable" refers to lossless transfer | "Business event as complete record"; debits/credits "one projection"; append-only not stated | `TestAppendOnlyInvariant` runs raw UPDATE/DELETE against event, rule, activity, bundle, object_type and fails if any succeeds |
| Replay reproduces identical state | Not applicable | "Re-upload the document… downstream accounting corrects on its own" (one record); no whole-ledger replay claim | "Wipe the cache, replay the log, compare" with `reflect.DeepEqual`, repeated in ~13 test files |

On verified evidence, the earlier conclusion holds. Numeric's FDP, read at its most generous, claims the first three dimensions in spirit and none of the last three; it is a strong "three of six", which is exactly the ceiling the earlier survey found everywhere else. No Numeric page says the event store is append-only, that rules carry versions pinned to the entries they produced, that past periods can be re-derived, or that state can be rebuilt from the log. Numeric's own July 2026 essay on AI audit trails lists "immutable timestamps and change history" and logging of "prompt, model version, and parameters" as questions buyers should ask vendors, and does not say Numeric's product does them ([Numeric blog, 2026-07-31](https://www.numeric.io/blog/ai-audit-trail)). The two senses in which Numeric uses "deterministic" are "code, not a model, computes the number" and "report logic is formulaic source code"; neither is a replay guarantee ([RightRev webinar recap, 2025-12-16](https://www.rightrev.com/agentic-finance-whats-real-whats-hype-what-to-implement/)).

Four things in the earlier report do change. First, its statement that no commercial AI-native ERP makes "the inversion (state as a function of log + rules)" is no longer true as a public claim: Numeric asserts precisely that inversion, and Gilbert's quote that "you can't reconstruct that picture from the trail of posted journal entries" is Rhea's provenance argument in a competitor's mouth ([Accounting Today, 2026-09-25](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-to-replace-erps)). Second, the finding that the authoring loop is commoditised now includes a ledger-replacement vendor whose slogan, "AI writes the rules. Rules do the accounting. … Models never compute a number or draft a journal entry", is word-for-word Rhea's AiRP line "AI authors, never executes" ([Numeric AI page](https://www.numeric.io/product/ai-in-numeric); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). Third, the earlier report said the funded category "competes on automation outcomes rather than architectural guarantees"; Numeric now competes on architecture by name ("ARCHITECTURE FINALLY BUILT FOR THE AGENT ERA", "same house, new paint" about Rillet and Campfire), so architectural vocabulary is saturated one level deeper than before ([Numeric FDP page](https://www.numeric.io/financial-data-platform)). Fourth, the earlier report argued incumbents "cannot retrofit it without a rewrite"; Numeric is evidence that a company with a shipping close product can put the inversion underneath it, at least as a claim, within a year of a Series B. The consequence for Rhea is sharp: the inversion is not the moat. What remains unowned is the verifiability of the inversion and the domain-as-data design, in which "the kernel never learns a domain" and "a market is a data file" ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). One inference deserves flagging rather than asserting: Numeric's pricing page describes "accounting policy enforcement through hard-coded workflows", the launch says auditors "can see and download source code", and App Studio "writes the code, and compiles it against your live financial data". Read together, these suggest that some AI-authored artifacts in the FDP are generated code rather than declarative data, which is the opposite of Rhea's invariant that "model output is data, never executed". Numeric does not say either way ([numeric.io pricing](https://www.numeric.io/pricing); [Numeric App Studio](https://www.numeric.io/product/app-studio)).

## What Numeric has that Rhea does not, and all of it is real

Every item in this section is verified by a primary source or by third-party data, except where marked as a vendor claim. Numeric has **a shipping product with a buyer**: the controller at a venture-backed US technology company of roughly 50 to 1,000 employees, running NetSuite, with a dedicated close team and a budget ([Audit Friendly](https://auditfriendly.co/compare/floqast-vs-numeric)). It has customers that will speak: Brex, Plaid, Wealthfront, OpenAI, Asana, Mercury, Public.com, DailyPay, Benchling, Riskified (NYSE-listed), GOAT, and more on the homepage logo bar ([TechCrunch, 2024-10-10](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/10/numeric-grabs-28m-series-a-for-automating-accounting-with-ai/); [numeric.io](https://www.numeric.io/)). It has **$89M of capital** across a $10M seed (2024-05), a $28M Series A led by Menlo (2024-10) and a $51M Series B led by IVP (2025-11), with angels including the former CEO of BlackLine and the former CFO of NetSuite, which names the two incumbents it intends to displace ([PR Newswire, 2025-11-19](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/numeric-raises-51m-series-b-expanding-from-close-management-to-comprehensive-finance-platform-302619774.html)). It has a team of roughly 100 to 120 people growing about 75% year on year, with no known layoffs ([TrueUp](https://partners.trueup.io/co/numeric-io); [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/private-companies/numeric)).

It has a **close-workflow surface** that Rhea does not address anywhere: checklist tasks with business-day due dates, dependencies, preparer and reviewer roles, templates reused each period, review notes that can block a period from closing, auditor read-only access, and a timestamped trail of every comment and attachment ([Numeric close page](https://www.numeric.io/product/close); [Numeric close-management solution](https://www.numeric.io/solutions/close-management-software)). Rhea's documents treat close as a period lock per book and month plus a worklist of unexplained events, and contain no checklist, no sign-off workflow, no flux narrative and no memo drafting ([DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md); [packs/pl/CORPUS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/CORPUS.md)). It has **integrations** that are independently verified: prebuilt connectors to NetSuite, Sage Intacct and QuickBooks Online and an open-API connection to Xero, plus Stripe, Brex, Ramp, Mercury, Salesforce, HubSpot, Snowflake, Databricks, Anrok, Carta and fourteen named banks ([ERP Research, checked 2026-08-20](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/financial-close/numeric); [Numeric integrations page](https://www.numeric.io/integrations)). Rhea's only shipped adapters are a clock and a KSeF client backed by a deterministic fake ([internal/adapter](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/adapter/)). It has **cash matching** with the only hard number in this entire comparison: a rules engine for one-to-one through many-to-many matches, AI-suggested rules from observed manual matches, and a claimed 90%+ automation rate, with Brex's rate reported rising "from about 30% to over 90%" in pilot — a vendor claim without independent benchmark, but one a customer put their name to ([Numeric cash matching](https://www.numeric.io/product/cash-matching); [PR Newswire, 2025-11-19](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/numeric-raises-51m-series-b-expanding-from-close-management-to-comprehensive-finance-platform-302619774.html)). Rhea's bank-to-book matching is a specimen distilled from a Polish SME suite and a parked view notion marked "later" ([SPEC.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/SPEC.md); [packs/pl/CORPUS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/CORPUS.md)).

It has compliance posture (SOC 1 and SOC 2 stated, SOC 1 Type 2 cited for the lossless ERP transfer), an open MCP server through which Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Gemini can read and write with the user's permissions, a Claude Skills library of eighteen templated close workflows, a technical-accounting assistant that drafts memos from the ASC codification, and billing and ASC 606 revenue recognition "off the same contracts and usage" ([Numeric data-foundation post](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-data-foundation-built-for-your-close-and-your-controls); [Numeric MCP post, 2026-04-20](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-mcp-server); [Numeric technical accounting assistant, 2026-04-01](https://www.numeric.io/blog/our-new-technical-accounting-assistant); [Numeric billing and revenue](https://www.numeric.io/product/billing-and-revenue)). And it has the one go-to-market idea Rhea lacks entirely: an on-ramp. Numeric lands as a close layer on NetSuite in month one, expands into cash and billing, and offers to "replace the GL when it's earned", running in parallel "for a close or two before switching" ([Numeric vs NetSuite](https://www.numeric.io/compare/numeric-vs-netsuite)). Rhea's intake "goes into the real log … No sandbox and no promotion step" ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)).

The caveats on Numeric's side are also real and should keep the fear proportionate. The FDP is two weeks old with one named customer. Independent reviewers rate the close product's compliance depth below FloQast's (62 vs 73), say "global multi-entity closes and legacy ERPs are out of scope today", and that "auditors may need more education than with incumbents" ([Audit Friendly](https://auditfriendly.co/compare/floqast-vs-numeric); [CFO Shortlist, 2026-10-06](https://www.cfoshortlist.com/vendors/numeric)). G2 reviewers report "frequent bugs and slow performance, particularly during busy periods and MEC refreshes" and integrations that "throw random error messages about failed task updates during the busiest MEC refresh days" ([G2 reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/numeric/reviews)). The customer-stories page still carries "Lorem ipsum" placeholder copy as of 2026-10-08 ([Numeric customer stories](https://www.numeric.io/customer-stories)). Numeric publishes automation rates but no accuracy, error or hallucination figures for any AI feature, and its own staff describe an AI-built report that "silently mixed local and consolidated currency" ([Numeric blog, 2026-03-18](https://www.numeric.io/blog/build-vs-buy-in-the-age-of-ai)).

## What Rhea has that Numeric does not: verified, and not

Rhea's advantages split cleanly into what a test proves today and what a document hopes for. The verified set is narrow and entirely architectural. The append-only invariant is enforced at the database: a test issues raw `UPDATE` and `DELETE` against the event, rule, activity, bundle and object-type tables and fails if any succeeds ([store_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/store/store_test.go)). Provenance is a constraint, not a convention: "a derived event without cause/rule violates the CHECK", every object field can be walked back to its root event in the shell, and the DuckDB projection carries the root event id ([store_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/store/store_test.go); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). Determinism is demonstrated the only way it can be: wipe the object cache, replay the log through the stored rule versions, compare with `reflect.DeepEqual`; the assertion recurs across the FX, parallel-books, amendment, late-event, activity, bundle, Germany, intercompany, cases, Poland, warehouse and clock test files ([exec_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/exec/exec_test.go)). The rule lifecycle is gated by tests, and the approval is itself an event with actor attribution ([exec_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/exec/exec_test.go); [activity_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/exec/activity_test.go)). The agent's containment is structural: the agent package has no store import and returns strict JSON that the kernel validates and rejects on bad output ([agent_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/agent/agent_test.go)). Money is `int64` minor units with FX converted at firing time in integer arithmetic "so replay never re-converts" ([core_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/core/core_test.go); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). The kernel's domain neutrality is proven negatively: warehousing landed with an empty `git diff` against the kernel, Germany ships zero object types, cases are pure data, and a test shows recurring schedules fail as pure data so that the clock primitive was earned rather than assumed ([warehouse_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/shell/warehouse_test.go); [cases_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/shell/cases_test.go); [schedule_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/exec/schedule_test.go)). Intercompany reconciliation is removed by construction and asserted: "every object on both sides provenance-chains to the same root event" ([intercompany_test.go](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/internal/shell/intercompany_test.go)). None of these has a public Numeric equivalent.

The unverified set is larger and is where the owner's fear should actually live. The network — the claim that knowledge learned at one installation makes every other wiser — exists as five evaluation runs of one model on one synthetic corpus, each publishing as its own "installation" into one local database; the DECISIONS entry itself attributes the convergence of 16 of 19 questions to the model being "consistent enough that shape fingerprints converge", which measures self-consistency rather than consensus between real businesses, and a line written the same day says "cross-client learning (the network) is designed, not built" ([DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md)). The "90% of companies in Poland do it like this" promise is recorded as a metaphor for what learning should make true ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). The AI evidence is two corpora totalling fifteen tasks scored against references the authors wrote: 9 of 9 on the first, which DECISIONS calls "too easy to discriminate"; 6 of 6 approved but "NOT DONE" on the operations corpus until late-understanding machinery landed, then DONE in two of three live runs; and the PZ warehouse task "failed in four of five runs on reading a field through a link" ([DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). Arithmetic does not exist: "value = quantity × price has no way in", so VAT from net times rate, due dates from payment terms and price-list valuation are all waiting on a formula language that has a brief but no design ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). The real KSeF API, the JPK writer and any bank-file adapter are absent; gapless statutory numbering is identified as kernel-level sequence state and not built; a 0% VAT invoice cannot post because conditional posting lines do not exist ([DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md); [packs/pl/CORPUS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/CORPUS.md)). The hash-chained log, the company partition key, authorisation and snapshot-plus-tail replay are named as production shapes and left for later ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). The host-and-operator experience ("I contacted the bank, booked 98 invoices, 2 are strange") has no code behind it ([vision-host-not-tool.md](/home/krzysztof/.claude/projects/-home-krzysztof-Rhea/memory/vision-host-not-tool.md)). And the whole thing has never been shown live to anyone, including its owner ([project-origins.md](/home/krzysztof/.claude/projects/-home-krzysztof-Rhea/memory/project-origins.md)).

## The Polish wedge contains no Numeric and only a thin Rhea

The geographic and segment facts are the least ambiguous part of this comparison. Numeric's only statutory function is US sales-tax resolution on invoices; its revenue module names ASC 606 and never IFRS 15; its consolidation guide reads eliminations "as configured in NetSuite rather than recalculated" and leaves currency translation to the ERP; ERP Research scores its tax provision and compliance at 0 of 4 and consolidation at 1 of 6; and searches for Numeric with VAT, KSeF, JPK, e-invoicing, IFRS or Poland return nothing from the company ([Numeric billing](https://www.numeric.io/product/billing); [Numeric consolidation guide, 2026-08-31](https://www.numeric.io/blog/multi-entity-consolidation-guide); [ERP Research](https://erpresearch.com/erp-add-ons/financial-close/numeric)). The July 2025 London office is a sales expansion into English-speaking, NetSuite-running, US-GAAP-adjacent teams; the most recent London posting is a business-development representative, engineering roles are in San Francisco, and no UK customer is named ([Numeric UK post, 2025-07-10](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-in-the-uk); [Built In London](https://builtinlondon.uk/company/numeric/jobs)). The ledgers a Polish SME actually runs (Comarch, Symfonia, enova, Optima) are not among Numeric's integrations and never will be the target of a NetSuite-replacement product ([Apideck Poland](https://www.apideck.com/integrations/country/poland/accounting)). Numeric also has no accounting-firm channel: it sells direct to in-house controllers, and no Big 4, mid-tier or bureau partner program was found ([Sage Intacct partners page, no Numeric listing](https://www.sage.com/en-us/sage-business-cloud/intacct/partners/)). The biura rachunkowe channel that Rhea's owner has named as the beachhead is one Numeric has not built and, at its price point, has no reason to build.

The segment gap is as wide as the geography. Numeric's close-product ideal customer is "$10M to $1B revenue", technology and fintech, "lean, technically comfortable accounting teams"; reviewers explicitly exclude companies under $10M revenue, companies without a dedicated close team, and "solo bookkeepers or very small businesses" ([CFO Shortlist, 2026-10-06](https://www.cfoshortlist.com/vendors/numeric); [BestAIAccounting, 2026-05](https://bestaiaccounting.com/reviews/numeric-review/)). The FDP targets higher still: Numeric's own competitive post says AI-native ERPs stop fitting "typically past $100M ARR", and that is where the FDP begins ([Numeric blog, 2026-02-27](https://numeric.io/blog/rillet-vs-campfire)). Contract data from 42 purchases puts the median at $15,000 a year with a floor above $4,000, and independent estimates run to $80,000 and beyond ([Vendr](https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/numeric)). Rhea's named audience is "thousands of smaller and bigger EU businesses", with the owner as the approving principal and the accountant as the future steward of the language, free of charge ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). The two populations do not overlap today. Numeric's economics exclude Rhea's segment; Rhea's segment does not run NetSuite, so Numeric's on-ramp has nothing to attach to.

The wedge is real, but Rhea occupies it thinly. The Polish pack is vocabulary and five rules: fourteen object types including `ksef_submission`, `bank_operation`, `settlement`, `fixed_asset` and `tax_declaration`, a chart of accounts and VAT rates as master-data events, plus a warehouse pack whose PZ receipt cites the Polish Accounting Act article 28 as its warrant and whose price-list valuation "waits for the formula language" ([packs/pl/pack.json](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/pack.json); [packs/pl/warehouse.json](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/warehouse.json)). The KSeF client is a deterministic fake by decision, there is no JPK writer and no bank-file adapter, and a VAT invoice cannot compute its own tax ([DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md); [project-origins.md](/home/krzysztof/.claude/projects/-home-krzysztof-Rhea/memory/project-origins.md)). The design does the right thing with statutory knowledge — a pack lands its rules as drafts for a human to activate, and "an adapter is a user, not a kernel extension" — but the empty space between a correct design and a Polish company's first month of real books is where all of the remaining work sits. Said plainly: Numeric cannot reach Rhea's segment without rebuilding its pricing, channel and statutory layer, and Rhea cannot serve that segment today either.

## What the FDP validates and what it threatens

The FDP validates more of Rhea's thesis than any previous public artefact, and it is worth saying what, precisely. It validates the **inversion itself** as a commercial proposition: a venture-backed company with hundreds of customers, an ex-NetSuite CFO as angel and $51M of fresh capital decided that the ledger should be a projection of stored business events, and said so to the trade press. IVP had already written in October 2024 that the GL and ERP market is "hundreds of times bigger than close automation alone" and that Numeric could "disrupt the general ledger and ERP market over time"; the FDP is that thesis executed, not a pivot ([IVP, 2024-10-10](https://ivp.com/content/numeric-using-AI-to-create-accounting-magic)). It validates the **division of labour**: "AI writes the rules. Rules do the accounting." is Rhea's AiRP claim restated by a company that has to defend it to auditors and SOX programs. It validates the **persona**: Numeric's launch targets "finance engineers, accountants who build systems that carry out accounting processes", and its NetSuite comparison promises that "controllers configure treatments, mappings, and policies themselves" rather than admins and consultants — which is Rhea's implementation-by-interview argument that "if implementing Rhea ever requires code, the ecosystem reverts to the old labour market" ([itbrief, 2026-09-28](https://itbrief.co.uk/story/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-to-replace-erps); [Numeric vs NetSuite](https://www.numeric.io/compare/numeric-vs-netsuite); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). It validates the **unit of interaction**: Prism, "the first screen you open in Numeric", is a daily digest of what agents did overnight and the exceptions that need a person, which is Rhea's host-and-operator morning question and its residue-zero definition of done ([Numeric Prism](https://www.numeric.io/product/prism); [vision-host-not-tool.md](/home/krzysztof/.claude/projects/-home-krzysztof-Rhea/memory/vision-host-not-tool.md)). It validates the **flywheel shape**: "Approve once and every record like it follows suit" and "AI agents observe your manual matches and suggest new rules" are rule-as-data learning loops, minus versioning and replay ([Numeric AI page](https://www.numeric.io/product/ai-in-numeric)). And, unintentionally, it validates Rhea's **strictness**: Numeric's own essay says "'A human reviews the output' is not a control", that evidence "reconstructed months later from a chat transcript that no longer exists is audit theater", and that a non-deterministic result needs prompt, model version and parameters to be reconstructed — a standard its product pages do not claim to meet, and one Rhea meets structurally by never letting the model into the execution path ([Numeric blog, 2026-07-31](https://www.numeric.io/blog/ai-audit-trail)).

The threats are real but mostly conditional, and it matters to keep them in that order. The one unconditional threat is **narrative ownership**. From this quarter on, "event-centric accounting", "data-centric accounting versus journal-entry accounting" and "the ledger as a projection" will be Numeric's phrases in the trade press, and any investor who reads Accounting Today will ask Rhea "how is this different from Numeric" before asking anything else. Rhea's public tagline, "Not AI on ERP. AiRP.", and Numeric's "same house, new paint" are the same rhetorical move, and Numeric's has a press release behind it ([rheamktg index](/home/krzysztof/rheamktg/public/index.html); [Accounting Today, 2026-09-25](https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/numeric-launches-financial-data-platform-to-replace-erps)). A second, structural threat is that the market may accept the weaker guarantee: if auditors come to treat an activity log plus source linkage as sufficient for AI-touched accounting, the commercial value of Rhea's stronger guarantee (replay as proof, provenance as constraint) shrinks, even if its technical value does not. Numeric's own warning that "the PCAOB has not endorsed any AI-assisted accounting workflow yet" means this question is open, and Numeric will be in the room when it is settled; Rhea will not ([Numeric blog, 2026-07-31](https://www.numeric.io/blog/ai-audit-trail)). A third threat is the **agent ecosystem**: Numeric's MCP server, used by 80% of adopting companies to check close status and by two-thirds to build reports, and its Claude Skills library, mean that third-party agents are being built on Numeric's data surface now ([Numeric MCP usage report, 2026-07-31](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-mcp-usage-report)). The conditional threats are EU localisation and a move down-market. Both would be needed before Numeric touches Rhea's segment; neither has any public evidence as of 2026-10-08, and the UK post's "tailoring our platform to local needs" is the only sentence pointing that way ([Numeric UK post, 2025-07-10](https://www.numeric.io/blog/numeric-in-the-uk)). The last threat is simply **scale of execution**: Numeric has roughly a hundred people and publishes features monthly; Rhea has one author and an AI pair, and every new business shape so far (PZ, carried links, arithmetic) has exposed a missing primitive ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)). None of these threats is the one the owner fears — that Rhea's idea was wrong or already done — but together they say that having the idea is no longer worth anything, and demonstrating it in a place Numeric cannot reach is worth everything.

## Four decisions, posed as business questions

**Should Rhea be the system of record, or sit beside the one a company already has?** Consider a Polish wholesaler on Comarch Optima with four hundred invoices a month and a bureau doing its books. Option A keeps Rhea's current design: Rhea replaces Optima at a year-end, and intake goes straight into the real log. Option B turns Rhea into what Numeric was in 2021: a layer that reads Optima's exports, proposes bookings and writes nothing back. Option C keeps Rhea as the system of record but adds Numeric's on-ramp: the client's KSeF invoices and bank statements enter Rhea as events while Optima keeps running, Rhea's trial balance is compared with Optima's export each month, and the switch happens when the two agree for two or three months. The recommendation is C. Option A has no adoption path at all, which is why there are no customers. Option B surrenders the ground this report says Rhea owns, because a layer cannot carry provenance for figures it did not derive. Option C is Numeric's own commercial lesson ("replace the GL when it's earned") applied to a segment Numeric does not serve, and Rhea's replay machinery turns "it matches the old books" from a feeling into a number ([Numeric vs NetSuite](https://www.numeric.io/compare/numeric-vs-netsuite); [DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)).

**Should Rhea build close features now that Numeric's are the benchmark?** Consider the accountant at month-end asking "what is left?" Option A builds a checklist, sign-offs and AI flux narratives in Numeric's image. Option B builds the bank-to-book reconciliation notion already specified from the Polish corpus — rules score candidate pairs by amount, NIP in the title, invoice number and date, a human confirms, confirmation emits a settlement event — and makes "residue zero plus period lock" the close. Option C builds nothing in this area. The recommendation is B. The checklist and the flux narrative are controller tools for a segment with a dedicated close team, which Rhea's segment does not have, and Numeric is years ahead there with a hundred people. Bank matching is different: it is the Polish SME's first daily pain in the corpus ("rule intent 1"), it is the one place where Numeric publishes a hard number to be measured against (90%+ automation, Brex 30% to over 90%), and it is the shape where Rhea's flywheel is visible — a human-confirmed match becomes a draft rule, the rule carries provenance, and the match rate is the first non-architectural metric Rhea would ever have ([packs/pl/CORPUS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/packs/pl/CORPUS.md); [Numeric cash matching](https://www.numeric.io/product/cash-matching)).

**How should Rhea talk about Numeric?** Consider the investor who has just read the FDP coverage. Option A ignores Numeric and keeps the "nobody occupies this ground" line. Option B names Numeric in the investor room's landscape page as the closest public analogue and states the difference. Option C positions against Numeric on public pages. The recommendation is B, within the owner's existing rules: competitors appear as a category on shareable pages, with the landscape page as the single named exception ([investor-positioning.md](/home/krzysztof/.claude/projects/-home-krzysztof-Rhea/memory/investor-positioning.md)). The page should say three things in order: that Numeric's FDP validates the event-first, AI-drafts-rules design at $89M of conviction; that Numeric has not claimed, and Rhea tests, append-only storage, replay, versioned rules and per-field provenance; and that Numeric sells to US companies past $100M ARR while Rhea's target is the EU small and mid business whose statute is a data pack. Option A will read as ignorance the first time an investor mentions Numeric; option C invites a comparison on shipped features that Rhea loses on every row.

**Does the formula session come before or after finding design partners?** Consider a Polish VAT invoice: net 1,000 zł at 23%. Rhea cannot compute the 230 zł today; the gross would have to arrive pre-computed, and the double-entry check would only refuse it if the three figures disagreed ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md); [DECISIONS.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DECISIONS.md)). Option A recruits partners first and lets their documents define the formula language. Option B ships arithmetic first and recruits after. Option C does both within the window, sequentially. The recommendation is C with the formula session first and partner conversations started in parallel, because a design partner's first real invoice would fail without arithmetic, while recruiting through an accounting office takes weeks of conversation that need no code. The constraint already recorded, that the formula VM "must be total — no loops, no I/O", is the right one and should not be relaxed under partner pressure ([DIRECTION.md](/home/krzysztof/Rhea/DIRECTION.md)).

## Conclusion

The useful thing Numeric's launch does is relocate Rhea's differentiation from an idea to a proof and a place. Before 2026-09-22 the owner could say nobody was building this; now a well-funded company says it is, and the only true remaining sentences are "nobody has shown it" and "nobody is doing it for a Polish company with twelve employees". Both are stronger claims than the old one, because both are demonstrable: the first by a recorded wipe-replay-compare and a test suite anyone can run, the second by a design partner's real books. There is also a quiet irony to carry into investor conversations. Numeric's own essay on AI audit trails sets a standard — immutable history, reconstructable results, review that is a control rather than a gesture — that its product pages do not claim to meet, and that Rhea meets by construction because the model never enters the path that produces a number. That is not a reason to feel safe; it is the one sentence Rhea should be able to prove on a screen within 90 days.

The verdict should be re-run on two triggers. If Numeric publishes its flows as a versioned, replayable format with provenance to a rule version, Rhea's mechanism advantage narrows to its market and its domain-as-data design. If Numeric announces continental-EU statutory support or a product priced for companies under $10M revenue, the segment gap narrows. Neither has happened; the owner's job is to make sure that by the time either does, Rhea is a working system in Polish hands rather than a thesis with excellent tests.
